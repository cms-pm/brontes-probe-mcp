# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers
at the email address listed in `pyproject.toml`. All complaints will be
reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

Project maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code of conduct in good
faith may face temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by the
project's leadership.
